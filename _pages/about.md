I am Yiyi Cai, a PhD student at Stanford Computer Science, advised by Adam Bouland and Mark Zhandry. I am grateful to be supported by a Stanford Graduate Fellowship and a NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. 

I am broadly interested in the interplay of theoretical computer science and quantum information, including quantum algorithms, coding theory, complexity theory, and cryptography. I hope to apply tools in TCS to discover relevant physical phenomena. 

Previously, I completed a B.S. in Electrical Engineering at Caltech, and a MPhil in Advanced Computer Science at the University of Cambridge as a [Gates-Cambridge Scholar](https://www.caltech.edu/about/news/yiyi_cai_gates_cambridge_scholarship).
